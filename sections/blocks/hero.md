# hero — the mark and the eyebrow above the title

**Type:** Hero (1). The quietest of the two hero kinds: it carries no words of its own beyond one short
label.

## What it actually draws

The project logo, centred, and an optional eyebrow line under it. That is all.

🔒 **It does NOT draw the page title.** The H1 is printed by the page factory — the same one that serves
posts, legal pages and the home page. Putting a title here would produce a second H1 on the page.

🔒 **The logo comes from settings, not from content.** Every project has its own and it changes in the
panel without a rebuild. Nothing uploaded — no logo is drawn, and that is honest: somebody else's mark
is worse than none.

## When to take it

The first screen of a page whose title is set by the page itself, and where you want the mark and a
short label above it. Neither logo nor eyebrow — the section renders nothing at all, so do not use it
as a spacer.

## When NOT to take it

- You need a real landing first screen with words and a picture side by side — that is `heroSplit`.
- You want a big statement — that is `statement`.
