# noBill — the invoices that will not come

**Type:** Pricing and plans (7). The only kind in this type today, and it argues about price from the
other side: not what you pay, but what you stop paying.

## What it actually draws

A named section: an H2 above, a list of other companies' names struck through with what you no longer
buy from them, and an H3 conclusion below.

🔒 **Two headings of different levels, and it is not a repetition.** The H2 on top NAMES the section:
without it the section stood in the middle of the page nameless — there were arguments, but the page did
not say whose. The H3 below is the CONCLUSION, and it has to come after the list: three struck-through
names are the evidence, the conclusion follows from them. Put the conclusion on top and the reader gets
a verdict before its grounds — that is asking him to take your word.

🔒 **The NAME is struck through, not the whole phrase.** A struck-through line "you do not pay Vercel"
reads as a cancelled statement — the exact opposite of what is meant.

## When to take it

The pricing part of a landing page where the argument is savings, not a price list.

## When NOT to take it

- You need plans with prices and a "popular" one: no kind does that yet — say so instead of bending
  this one.
- The list is your own features, not somebody else's invoices — that is `cards` or `badges`.
