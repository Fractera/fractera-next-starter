# projectTypeMarquee — the ribbon of directions

**Type:** Trust and logos (10). Deliberately the same breed of section as the language ribbon.

## What it actually draws

A ribbon of twenty-two cards naming what can be built on this product — from a landing page to a company
brain — each with one line saying how it differs from its neighbour.

🔒 **What it proves.** "Build anything" is a claim that means nothing. The ribbon shows the specifics in
the face.

🔒 **The directions come from the CATALOGUE, not from page content.** The list lives in
`config/project-types.ts` and is the same one the owner picks his project structure from in the panel. A
second copy inside a language cell would drift from the panel on the first direction added.

## When to take it

A page that has to answer "what is this even for" with breadth rather than depth.

## When NOT to take it

- Right next to `languageMarquee`: two ribbons in a row read as one long ribbon, and both stop proving
  anything.
- When the visitor already knows what he is building — then breadth is noise.
